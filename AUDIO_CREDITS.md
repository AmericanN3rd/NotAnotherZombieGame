# Audio credits

The sound effects below are CC0 (public domain). Each of those licenses was checked on the OpenGameArt source page, which links [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required for them. Gunshots, the knife, reloads, and footsteps stay synthesized in the game and are not listed here. The lobby theme is not CC0; it is credited under Original music by the user.

`rain.ogg` and `thunder.ogg` are trims of the same recording: the bed is the opening rain, and the thunder one-shot is the strike later in that file.

A dedicated teeth-grinding recording with a verified CC0 license was not found. `teeth.ogg` is a CC0 bone break/snap, used as the grinding layer in the intro. Wall scratching is a CC0 scrape, not a fingernail foley take.

## Original music by the user

| File | Credit |
| --- | --- |
| `music/camp.ogg` | Basecamp theme, original music by American Nerd, all rights reserved by the author |

`camp.ogg` plays in the lobby, loops, and crossfades when a climb starts. It is not fetched or decoded while the app is booting. Climb and hatch are still the CC0 placeholders above. Dropping `climb.ogg` or `hatch.ogg` (or `.m4a` / `.mp3` with the same base name) replaces those slots. If several extensions share a name, `.ogg` is the one that loads.
